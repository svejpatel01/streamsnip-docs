Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

StreamSnip is a personal, single-user tool built and operated by Svej Patel. It is not a public service or company — it runs locally on the developer's own computer and is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

What it does

StreamSnip reads publicly available Twitch clip data (via the Twitch API) and reformats short video clips, then posts them to TikTok accounts the developer personally owns and manages, using TikTok's Content Posting API.

What data is accessed

  • Twitch: public clip metadata (title, view count, creation time) and clip video files, via Twitch's Helix API.
  • TikTok: basic account info (display name) and video-publishing access, via TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, authorized directly by the account owner through TikTok's own OAuth login.

Where data is stored

All data — TikTok access/refresh tokens, a short-lived log of posted clips, and account records — is stored locally in a SQLite database file on the developer's own computer. Nothing is transmitted to any third party or server other than Twitch's and TikTok's own APIs, which are required to operate the tool.

Data retention

Downloaded and processed video files are deleted immediately after each post attempt. The log of previously posted clips is kept for 30 days for de-duplication purposes, then automatically deleted.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or made available to any third party. This tool has no other users besides its developer.
